# IDLE DIGITAL — FINAL VISUAL ART DIRECTION

Refine the existing Idle Digital website.

DO NOT change the approved information architecture, business positioning, service structure, plans structure, About messaging, Contact flow, or overall content strategy unless explicitly instructed below.

This update is primarily an ART DIRECTION and VISUAL SYSTEM redesign.

The existing website still feels too much like a dark SaaS / AI technology website.

Move away from:

* glowing dashboard UI
* cybersecurity-style grids
* concentric tech rings
* excessive dark backgrounds
* generic SaaS cards
* overly futuristic interfaces
* tiny floating dashboards everywhere
* blue used only as a glow

Idle Digital should instead feel like:

A bold, premium, contemporary subscription-based digital studio.

Think:

EDITORIAL DESIGN
+
PRODUCTIZED CREATIVE SERVICE
+
EXPRESSIVE DIGITAL MOTION
